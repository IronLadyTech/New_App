# LEP — what your tasks are (participant guide)

This guide explains **every kind of task** in the Leadership Essentials Program (LEP), in plain language. It is based on:

- **LEP Program Journey v1.5** (calendar: Orientation Thursday → Day 1 Saturday → Day 2 Sunday → four follow-up weeks)
- **`LEP-Messages-Complete-v6.xlsx`** — “LEP task guide” (54 LMS tasks) + task types and due messaging
- **`docs/LEP_T01_T27_Mandatory_Optional.xlsx`** — official **mandatory vs optional** rules for certificate

In the **Iron Lady app**, tasks appear under **Learn / My program** (and on your **home** “today” list). What you see unlocks with your **batch dates** (for example, **5 Daily Rituals** only after the intensive weekend, from **Monday of Week 1**).

---

## What you see **inside each task** (app screen)

When you tap a task, the screen is always built the same way. Look for these blocks **in order**:

| # | On screen | What it means |
|---|-----------|----------------|
| 1 | **Kind** (small label) | Form · Link · Checklist · Assignment · Video · Write |
| 2 | **Title + description** | Why this task exists |
| 3 | **Why this matters** (pink card) | **Purpose** — why we ask for this task |
| 4 | **What you'll have when you're done** | **Outcome** — what you gain (profile, strategy, certificate step, etc.) |
| 5 | **How to complete it · …** (beige card) | **Numbered steps** — video, NSDC link, form, upload (not “read PDF” as the only message) |
| 6 | **Batch materials** | PDFs to read in-app, or links (VIA website, templates) |
| 7 | **Watch first** (if shown) | Video player for 27 Principles, 5 Rituals, brand session, etc. |
| 8 | **Your action** | Checklist ticks · worksheet fields · link box · file upload · record video |
| 9 | **Submit** | Mark watched · Submit form · Submit link · Submit file · Submit video |

Copy for **why / what you gain** lives in `constants/lepTaskPurpose.js` (from the Excel **LEP task guide**). **How** stays in the beige steps card.

**Examples of the “Inside this task” line:**

- **NSDC** — register on the government portal → copy confirmation link → paste in the app → **Submit link**.
- **VIA** — complete survey on viacharacter.org → save PDF → **upload report + 3 photos**.
- **Form** — open PDF guide if listed → fill worksheet → **Submit form**.
- **Video pitch** — read prep PDF → record 60–120 sec → **Submit video** (certificate must-do).

The tables below use the same categories. In the app, the exact steps for **your** task are always in the **Inside this task** card on that task’s page.

## How to read a task in the app

| Label in this guide | What you do | Examples |
|---------------------|-------------|----------|
| **Watch (video)** | Press play in the app; finish the session | 27 Principles, 5 Rituals, brand/mentors videos |
| **Read (PDF)** | Open the document, read, tick “done” or confirm | Program Framework, ERRC template intro, Crucibles guide |
| **Fill in (form)** | Type answers in the app worksheet and **Submit** | Beliefs, ERRC table, CoDeSeF, Purpose Peg, responding exercise |
| **Upload** | Attach a file (PDF, photo, or short video) | VIA report + photos, Day 1/2 assignments, resume, Shameless Pitch |
| **External link** | Complete on **another website**, then paste link or upload proof in the app | VIA Character survey, **NSDC** registration, LinkedIn profile URL |
| **Daily check-in** | Same short task **every day** (often ~2–18 min) | A-Game Table, **5 Daily Rituals** tracker |
| **Short write-up** | A few lines or a structured text task | Mentors list, Progress review, Guiding Stars, weekly Challenge |
| **Feedback / engagement** | Quick form after a live session | “How did Day 1–2 land?”, weekly engagement forms |
| **Quiz** | Multiple-choice in the app | Rituals quiz, 27 Principles quiz |
| **Admin (not “homework”)** | Payments, attendance, certificate | Balance payment (Sales), attendance record, certification |

**Due dates:** Your batch calendar sets the real dates. CEM messaging often uses **“before Day 1 (Saturday)”** for pre-work and **Friday of that week** for the three certificate must-dos (see below).

## The only three must-dos for certificate (everything else is optional)

From the finalized LEP rules (T01–T27 sheet):

| Must-do | What it is | When it is due |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| **B-HAG** | **Part A:** B-HAG form when you enroll. **Part B:** same big goal written into your **CoDeSeF** sheet by end of Week 1 | Pre-program **Friday** (form) · Week 1 **Friday** (in CoDeSeF) |
| **VIA Survey** | Take the free strengths survey **outside** the app, upload your report (+ **3 photographs** in the LMS task) | Pre-program **Friday** |
| **Shameless Pitch** | About **60 seconds** — your Need, Differentiation, Offer (often a short **video** upload) | Week 2 **Friday** |

All other learning tasks are **optional** for moving forward: they do not “lock” the next step or turn your status red. Your CEM may still nudge you because they help you get the most from live sessions.

**Not learning homework:** pending **program payment** (admin/Sales), **NSDC** (government registration — still important for certification paperwork), **attendance** on live days.

## Your month at a glance

```text
Pre-program     →  VIA, CoDeSeF draft, NSDC, B-HAG, profile info
Orientation Thu →  Read framework, access checklist, block dates in your calendar
Day 1 Sat       →  Live + beliefs, ERRC, crucibles, Day 1 assignment (forms/uploads)
Day 2 Sun       →  Live + CoDeSeF, responding, relationships, Purpose Peg, Day 2 work
Week 1 Mon+     →  5 Rituals daily + 27 Principles video + mentors + roadmap
Week 2          →  LinkedIn, resume, Shameless Pitch (must-do)
Week 3          →  Progress review, Guiding Stars
Week 4          →  Graduation prep, alumni, quizzes, journey deck (optional)
```

Live sessions (Zoom links in the app) are the **anchor**; tasks are how you **apply** what happened in the room.

## Practical tips

1. **Pre-program:** Finish **VIA**, **B-HAG**, and **NSDC** before Day 1 Saturday if you can; add **CoDeSeF** draft before Day 2.
2. **Weekend intensive:** After each live day, do **one** task the same evening while it is fresh (messages suggest Beliefs → ERRC → Crucibles → Day 1 assignment).
3. **Monday after the weekend:** Start **5 Rituals** the same day the app unlocks them; watch the **5 Rituals video** once.
4. **Certificate focus:** Only **three** learning items are strictly mandatory; still show up live and complete **NSDC** so admin is smooth.
5. **Stuck?** Reply to your CEM WhatsApp — message templates in the Excel “Task messages” sheet are written for exactly these blockers.
